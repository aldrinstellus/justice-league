## Mission Naming Convention

**Format**: `JL-XXX-mission-name`

**Rules**:
- Sequential numbering (001, 002, 003...)
- Never reuse mission IDs
- Use kebab-case for mission names
- Keep names concise (3-5 words max)

**Examples**:
- `JL-001-tweakcn-research`
- `JL-002-auzmor-learn-figma`
- `JL-003-component-migration`
